Why active learning?

Meta-analyses show that active learning improves student performance, reduces failure rates, and narrows equity gaps across STEM and non-STEM courses.

  • Increases student engagement and retention
  • Supports inclusive pedagogy
  • Scales from small seminars to large lectures

How to get started

  1. Identify your learning outcomes
  2. Choose 1–2 strategies aligned to outcomes
  3. Plan assessment and feedback loops
