# Jigsaw Kit

## Roles
- Facilitator
- Scribe
- Timekeeper
- Reporter

## Timing (example 45–60 min)
- Expert groups learn subtopic (15–20)
- Home groups teach and assemble (20–25)
- Class synthesis (5–10)

## Prompts
- Subtopic goals and guiding questions
- Deliverable for home group
- Quick check-for-understanding
